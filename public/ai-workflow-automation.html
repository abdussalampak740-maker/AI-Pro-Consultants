What is AI Workflow Automation?

Standard automation (like Zapier) moves data from A to B. **AI Workflow Automation** (using tools like n8n or Make.com + LLMs) *processes* that data before it arrives at B.

It's the difference between "Email received -> Save attachment" and "Email received -> AI reads attachment -> AI Categorizes expense -> AI Flags anomalies -> AI Updates accounting software."

The 3 Pillars of AI Automation

1. The Data Bridge

Connecting your tech stack (Gmail, Slack, CRM, Airtable) into a single logical "brain."

2. The Cognitive Step

Inserting a Large Language Model (GPT-4 or Claude) into the flow to perform reasoning, summarization, or translation.

3. The Action Trigger

Closing the loop by having the system perform a real-world task—like drafting an invoice, sending a Slack alert, or updating a lead status.
